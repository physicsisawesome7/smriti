# Code of conduct

Smriti is built by people from government, engineering and software backgrounds, in many languages. We want everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions, events and private messages about the project) to be able to do so without harassment or fear.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Be patient with newcomers and with people writing in a second language.
- Give feedback that is specific and kind, and accept it the same way.
- Credit other people's work.
- Respect privacy: do not share anyone's personal information, or the content of documents you have access to at work, without permission.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, intimidation, threats or personal attacks.
- Insults or demeaning comments about anyone's identity, including caste, religion, gender, sexuality, disability, age, nationality or appearance.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information (doxxing).
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or encouraging any of the above.

## Reporting

Write to **[lalit@safearly.com](mailto:lalit@safearly.com)** with "Code of conduct" in the subject. Tell us what happened, where, and who was involved, with links or screenshots if you have them. Only the maintainers handling the report will see it. We will acknowledge it within 3 working days.

## What happens next

Maintainers may, depending on how serious the behaviour is:

1. Send a private, written warning.
2. Remove or edit comments, commits or issues.
3. Ban the person from the project for a fixed period.
4. Ban them permanently.

People reporting in good faith will not face retaliation.

## Scope and attribution

This code applies in every project space, and when someone represents the project in public. It is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
